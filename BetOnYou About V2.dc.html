ABOUT BETONYOU

Our story.

It started with a promise to get healthy. The change didn't come from a plan or an app. It came from someone who refused to let me quit.

BetOnYou is that kitchen-table bet, rebuilt for everyone.

Nine years ago, a doctor slid my lab results across the desk and told me the numbers were going the wrong way — weight, blood pressure, the quiet beginnings of type 2. I knew exactly what to do. Everyone does. I'd known for a decade. Knowing was never the problem.

What finally worked wasn't a plan or an app. It was my sister. She put $200 on the kitchen table and said: sixty days, a walk every morning, photo proof in the family chat. Finish and the money's yours; quit and it goes to charity. My niece asked about it every Sunday. Suddenly the walk wasn't a private negotiation at 6 AM — it was a promise to people I couldn't stand to disappoint.

Sixty days became a habit. The habit became forty pounds, then a normal A1C, then — stranger and better — my brother-in-law quitting cigarettes on his own challenge, funded by the same family pot. Change didn't spread through lectures. It spread through belief, with money on it.

Think of it like a fundraiser, except the goal is a person: quitting smoking, losing the weight, staying sober, making every checkup, or winning an office step war nobody wants to lose. Anyone can start a challenge — for themselves or for someone they love. Believers add to the pot, proof gets verified, and the payout lands only when the goal is met.

We measure success in finished challenges, and in the people watching from the couch who quietly decide they're next.

— The BetOnYou team

01 · WHAT WE BELIEVE

Three ideas we built the whole thing on

Belief is a resource

When someone puts money behind you, they're saying "I know you can" in the most concrete language there is. We make that easy to say — and hard to walk back.

Bet on behavior, not outcomes

Challenges reward showing up — the walk, the check-in, the smoke-free day — because repeated behavior is what actually changes lives, not a number on a scale.

Health is a team sport

Lasting health is built from routines, food, checkups, transport and people — not willpower alone. So we built BetOnYou for families, friends, coworkers and communities.
