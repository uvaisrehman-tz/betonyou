Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. BetOnYou is built on trust — the same trust your believers place in you — so we keep data handling clear and minimal.

01Overview

BetOnYou ("we", "us") operates an accountability platform where people stake money on personal goals, invite believers to back them, and unlock a pot when AI-verified proof shows they followed through. This policy applies to our website, apps, and related services.

By using BetOnYou you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

02Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run challenges and keep payouts honest:

  • Account details — name, email, and profile information you provide.
  • Challenge data — goals, stakes, deadlines, and the believers you invite.
  • Proof submissions — photos, videos, GPS routes, and activity data you upload as evidence.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment partners; we never store full card numbers.
  • Usage data — device, browser, and interaction data used to improve the product.

03How we use your information

Your information is used to build and adapt your challenge plan, verify proof, move funds between you and your believers, send timely reminders, and keep the platform secure.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your proof submissions to train third-party models without your explicit consent.

04Proof & verification data

Proof is the heart of BetOnYou, so we treat it carefully. Submissions are analyzed by our verification system to confirm they are real and recent, then stored only as long as needed to resolve the challenge and any disputes.

You control what your believers see. They receive the progress you choose to share — never your raw files or location history.

05Sharing with believers & third parties

We share limited information with the believers on your challenge (your progress and milestones) and with service providers who help us operate — payment processors, cloud hosting, and analytics — under strict confidentiality.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

06Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After you close your account, we delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or accounting reasons.

07Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

  • Access and download your data from account settings.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

08Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular reviews to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep yours safe and notify you promptly of any incident that affects you.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be highlighted in-app or by email, and the "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.

10Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at privacy@betonyou.com and we'll get back to you.
