01 · HOW IT COACHES

Coaching that knows your challenge.

It reads the goal you set, the proof you submit, and the way you actually behave — then adapts. No two people get the same coach.

Understands your goal

Lose 10kg, quit smoking, ship an MVP, read three books — the coach parses what you're chasing and builds a plan around the behavior that gets you there.

Adapts to your rhythm

Miss two mornings in a row? It shortens the target and moves the reminder. On a hot streak? It raises the bar. The plan bends so you don't break.

Verifies your proof

Photo, video, GPS route or activity data — checked in seconds against your goal, so a milestone only counts when it's real. Believers trust the payout.

Nudges like a human

Timed, warm, and specific — never a spammy streak alert. It knows the difference between a gentle reminder and the push you need before you quit.
